# Surface: Maktub invitation (public page /i/{slug} rendered with template "maktub")

Mode: Experience — the guest is inside the invitation itself; the work leads from the first viewport.
Audience: wedding guests of every age opening a Telegram link on a phone; job: feel the occasion, learn when/where, answer RSVP.
Constraints: brief-pinned world (cream, burgundy wax seal, gold lines; tapping the seal opens the envelope and the letter comes out). Original SVG/CSS graphics only, no raster/video. Latin + Cyrillic. Text ≥16px, targets ≥44px. Reduced motion respected. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone is the letter. The guest breaks a real-feeling wax seal, the flap swings open on its burgundy liner, and the sheet that slides out IS the page — one continuous laid-paper letter read top to bottom. Refuses the category default: a stack of centred beige cards with a photo hero.

OWN-WORLD: Laid cream paper (#F4ECDD, chain lines + fibre noise), ink brown-black text, burgundy wax (#7A1426 → #4A0A16) with lit 3D relief, gold foil used only as hairlines, girih 8-point-star ornament and islimi scroll corners. The one saturated field: deep burgundy velvet ground and the envelope liner with gold khatam lattice. Type: Great Vibes calligraphy for names and section titles, EB Garamond for the letter body, Forum capitals for small labels.

STORY: Addressed by name on the envelope → breaks the seal → reads who, the date hand-circled in red ink on the month, the countdown → the programme as a gold thread down the margin → venue with map buttons → gift → fills the reply card in ink and seals it with a stamp → closing signed by the families.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Portrait envelope (≈86vw, flap tip at ~52% height) centred on burgundy velvet; wax seal (~30% envelope width) with initials at the flap tip, softly pulsing gold ring; "Hurmatli {ism}" handwritten on the lower flap; Forum caption "Ochish uchun muhrni bosing" under it; Lotin/Кирилл switch top-right. Primary action = the seal.

FORM: Brief-pinned object world (letter in a sealed envelope), position 1 of 1; no roll. seed: none (brief-pinned direction, concept-seed not run)
Signature interaction: seal cracks with a gold light leak → halves split → flap rotates on rotateX revealing liner → letter rises → envelope falls away, letter becomes the page. Paired with the reply: submitting RSVP presses a wax stamp onto the reply card. Motion grammar: ink and wax — the day ring draws itself, the programme thread grows; no generic per-section entrances.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
